     - Call `renpy.restart_interaction()` on toggle and on socket connection.
     - Removed blocking `config.afm_callback`.

- Diagnosed and fixed auto-forward mode becoming unresponsive when game was left minimized for hours:
   1. Root Causes:
      - When minimized/idle for hours, Windows audio device standby or PortAudio stream suspension can stall `stream.write` or delay `PLAYBACK_DONE`.
      - `tts_state.is_playing` remained stuck as `True` with no timeout or watchdog, blocking `tts_toggle_auto()` and `TTSAutoAdvanceAction` from ever advancing.
      - Keymap only recognized lowercase `'a'`; pressing with CapsLock or Shift was ignored by Ren'Py's `Keymap`.
      - If socket disconnected during idle/sleep, `tts_state.is_playing` was not reset to False.
      - `KokoroEngine` lacked a dedicated `stop()` implementation to drain queued audio and unblock playback threads.
   2. Fixes implemented in `renpy_mod/tts_mod.rpy` and `renpy_mod/tts_engines.py`:
      - Added `speak_start_time` and dynamic `expected_duration` calculation based on text length and playback speed.
      - Watchdog in `TTSAutoAdvanceAction`: if auto is active and playback has been stuck beyond expected duration, forces `is_playing = False` and triggers auto-advance.
      - Auto toggle (`tts_toggle_auto`): if audio expired or stuck (e.g. game was minimized for hours), resets `is_playing = False` and advances immediately.
      - Expanded keybindings to `'a'`, `'A'`, `'shift_K_a'`, `'K_a'` and synchronized `config.keymap["toggle_afm"]`.
      - Ensured socket close/error in `_tts_socket_listener` resets `is_playing = False`.
      - Implemented `stop()` in `KokoroEngine` to drain queue, signal `stop_flag = True`, and reset audio stream on error.
   3. TDD: Added 5 new unit tests in `tests/test_renpy_startup.py`.
